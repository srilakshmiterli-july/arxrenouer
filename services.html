FAQs

Common Questions

How are your fees set?

Most projects use a flat, phase-based design fee based on scope. Consultations and extra work are billed hourly. Furniture and finishes are offered at designer pricing.

Where do you work?

We are based in Frisco, Texas, and serve the Dallas–Fort Worth area, including Plano, McKinney, Prosper, Allen, and Dallas.

What happens at the first consultation?

We listen to your goals, look at your space or plans, talk about budget and timeline, and explain how we would approach your project.

Do I need a contractor?

For construction work, yes. We coordinate with your contractor, or can recommend trusted partners, and stay involved through installation.
