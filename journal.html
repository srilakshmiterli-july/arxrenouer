The Journal

From Story to Space · How a concept shapes every detail

Concept · Residential · By Srilakshmi Terli

At Arx Renouer, every project starts the same way — not with furniture or finishes, but with a story. Before we draw a single line, we ask how our clients live, work, and gather, and what they want to feel in their space. That story becomes our design concept, and the concept guides every decision that follows.

The story: a world to explore

Cameron’s Canyon Studio was designed for James Cameron, a filmmaker and explorer who spends his life creating new worlds for others. So the concept turned that story around: this site is not just a site — it is an experience. Instead of creating a world, here he is given a world to explore.

How the story becomes space

The red-rock canyon becomes the new world, and the studio becomes the lens through which he discovers it. Floor-to-ceiling glass frames the landscape like scenes in a film. On the ground level, collaboration and storyboarding spaces open onto the pool terrace and the canyon beyond; above, a quieter director’s studio and screening room look out over it. Moving through the house becomes a journey of discovery.

From concept to reality

Once the concept was clear, we carried it through design development, coordinated drawings, and implementation, so every detail had a job. A sculptural black spiral stair links the two levels and becomes the centerpiece of the double-height living space. Warm wood lines a quiet lounge niche, balancing the cool concrete and dark metal. Even the lighting — a cluster of glowing globes — echoes the idea of light on a screen.

Your story, your space

Whether it’s a family home, a café, or a senior living community, the best spaces are the ones that feel made for the people inside them. Thinking about a new project? Book a consultation — we’d love to hear your story.

Cameron’s Canyon Studio is a design studio project completed through UC Berkeley.
