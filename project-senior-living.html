Serenity Senior Living

Reception desk with sage green feature wall and wood slat paneling
Lobby — curved seating, biophilic plantings, and welcome wall mural
Library — reading nooks with botanical mural and indoor plantings
Library connecting to the adjacent lobby space
Dining room — coral booth seating with botanical mural and planter dividers
Presentation board — concept, adjacency diagram, and furnished floor plan

Course: Space Planning and Programming | UC Berkeley

Role: Student Designer

Facility Size: ~12,500 SF

Concept

Serenity Senior Living draws from the biophilic principle — connecting residents to natural light, organic textures, and serene greenery. Circulation flows from public to private like a quiet retreat: arrival → community → dwelling. Natural materials — warm wood, stone, and woven textiles — are layered with soft greenery to blur the line between inside and outside.

Spatial Organization

Public Core → Acoustic Buffer → Private Ring

Focus Areas

The lobby and reception welcome residents with curved seating and biophilic plantings. The library offers high-back reading nooks with botanical murals and indoor greenery. The dining room features warm coral booth seating with planter dividers and pendant lighting. Universal design and accessibility are woven throughout — high-contrast edges, chairs with arms, clear circulation, and cognitive wayfinding.

Design Approach

Evidence-based material selection supports a calm, durable, biophilic senior-living environment. Warm oak LVT, terrazzo porcelain, sage feature walls, walnut slat acoustic panels, slip-resistant floors, cleanable textiles, and low-VOC finishes create spaces that prioritize resident wellbeing, comfort, and dignity.
